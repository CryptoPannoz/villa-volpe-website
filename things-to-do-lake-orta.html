Explore Lake Orta

Things to Do on Lake Orta

An interactive map of our favourite places around the lake — from Orta San Giulio and its island to waterfalls, hikes, watersports and the best tables. Hand-picked by the hosts of Villa Volpe.

Lake Orta is the quiet, romantic little sister of the bigger Italian lakes: a UNESCO-listed sacred mountain, a car-free medieval village, a monastery on an island, and green hills laced with trails and waterfalls. Everything below is within an easy drive of Villa Volpe. Use the category filters to plan your days, and tap any place on the map or card to learn more.

🏛️ Villages, islands & landmarks

🏛️ Village

Orta San Giulio

The car-free medieval heart of the lake: cobbled lanes, frescoed houses and Piazza Motta opening straight onto the water.

Read the guide
🏛️ Island

San Giulio Island

A tiny island crowned by a Romanesque basilica and a silent monastery, a two-minute boat ride from Orta's jetty.

The legend of San Giulio
🏛️ UNESCO site

Sacro Monte di Orta

A UNESCO World Heritage hill above the village, with twenty frescoed chapels dedicated to St Francis and sweeping lake views.

🏛️ Sanctuary

Madonna del Sasso

A baroque sanctuary perched on a granite spur, offering arguably the finest panorama over the whole of Lake Orta.

🏛️ Museum

Umbrella & Parasol Museum

In hillside Gignese, the only museum in the world devoted to umbrellas and parasols — quirky, charming and rain-proof.

🌿 Nature, waterfalls & viewpoints

🌿 Waterfall

Cascata della Qualba

A hidden waterfall with an ice-cold natural plunge pool near Boleto — a refreshing reward after a short forest walk.

Cold plunge guide
🌿 Waterfall

Cascata del Pescone

Cascades and green pools along the Pescone stream near Ameno — a lovely spot to cool off on a hot day.

🌿 Viewpoint

Torre di Buccione

A 23-metre medieval watchtower on a wooded knoll, reached by a short walk and rewarding you with a wide view over the southern lake.

🌿 Walk

Monte Mesma Convent

A 15th-century Franciscan convent crowning a quiet, chestnut-covered hill — an easy walk to peace and panorama.

🌿 Hike

Monte Falò & Tre Montagnette

An easy ridge hike above the eastern shore with a string of panoramic viewpoints over lake and Alps.

🌿 Trail

Indigo Ring — WWF Oasis of Ameno

A circular nature trail through the WWF oasis, woodland and evocative abandoned hamlets in the hills of Ameno.

🌿 Viewpoint

Cappella del Vago

A tiny hilltop chapel and one of the loveliest, least-crowded sunset viewpoints over the lake.

🚴 Outdoor activities & watersports

🚴 On the water

Kayak, Canoe & SUP

Rent a kayak, canoe or stand-up paddleboard on the Orta lakefront and glide out to the island under your own steam.

Outdoor activities
🚴 On the water

Sailing on Lake Orta

Learn to sail or head out on a skippered cruise across the calm, sheltered waters of the lake.

Sailing with Dodo Gorla
🚴 Adrenaline

Wakeboard & Wakesurf

Wake&More runs wakeboard and wakesurf sessions for all levels on the quieter southern reach of the lake.

Wakeboarding guide
🚴 On two wheels

E-Bike Rental & Tours

Hire an e-bike and let the motor flatten the hills as you loop the lake or climb to a viewpoint.

Outdoor activities
🚴 Family fun

Adventure Park Le Pigne

Treetop rope courses and zip-lines in the woods above the lake — a great half-day for families and thrill-seekers.

🚴 Mountain

Mottarone & Alpyland

The 1,491 m peak between Orta and Maggiore, with an alpine coaster, adventure park and a 360° view of seven lakes.

🍽️ Where to eat & drink

🍽️ Fine dining ★★

Villa Crespi

Antonino Cannavacciuolo's two-Michelin-star restaurant in a fairy-tale Moorish villa — the lake's flagship gastronomic experience.

The Villa Crespi experience
🍽️ Fine dining ★

Al Sorriso

A historic Michelin-starred kitchen in nearby Soriso, a serene 15-minute drive from the lake.

🍽️ Lakeside

Laqua by the Lake

Cannavacciuolo's relaxed lakeside restaurant and resort at Pettenasco, right on the water.

🍽️ Lakefront

Hotel San Rocco

A refined lakefront restaurant with a pool terrace in the heart of Orta — lovely for a special dinner over the water.

🍽️ Agriturismo

La Darbia

A hillside agriturismo with its own vineyard, seasonal cooking and a memorable view down to the lake.

🍽️ Trattoria

Taverna Antico Agnello

A cosy trattoria in Orta's old town serving generous Piedmontese classics.

🍽️ Osteria

L'Ustaria Cà dal Rat

A rustic osteria in the hills above the lake, strong on local dishes and a warm welcome.

🍽️ Restaurant

Hotel Bocciolo

A friendly hotel restaurant in the centre of Orta San Giulio, handy for lunch between sights.

🍽️ Lakeside

Ristorante Imbarcadero

Lake fish and a waterside terrace by the jetty at Pella, on the sunnier western shore.

🍽️ Panoramic

La Rampolina

A panoramic restaurant on the hills above the lake, worth the drive for the view alone.

🍽️ Gelato

Gelateria Antica Torre

Artisan gelato on the western shore at Pella — the perfect excuse for a lakeside stroll.

🍽️ Café & pastry

Pasticceria Arianna

A neighbourhood pastry shop and café for a proper Italian breakfast a short stroll from the villa.
