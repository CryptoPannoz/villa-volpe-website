A day like a local

One perfect day, card by card

  1. 09:30

    🚲Pick up an e-bike on Via Domodossola, ten minutes on foot from the villa, and ride the lakeside road.

  2. 11:00

    ⛴️From Piazza Motta, the public motorboat to San Giulio island: two minutes across, a whole other world.

  3. 13:00

    🍝Lunch in the village at Filo e Fieno: handmade pasta, closed on Tuesdays.

  4. 16:00

    🏄Summer afternoon: wakesurf with Wakeorta in Pettenasco, five minutes by car.

  5. 18:30

    🍷Aperitivo at Pan & Vino on Piazza Motta: local wine, cheese and salumi at the tables.

  6. 20:30

    🌙Dinner on the water at Sant'Antonio, a twenty-minute walk from the villa.

Check the opening days: some places close on Tuesdays or Wednesdays, and the lake sports run in summer only.

🏛️ Villages, islands & landmarks

🏛️ Village

Orta San Giulio

The car-free medieval heart of the lake: cobbled lanes, frescoed houses and Piazza Motta opening straight onto the water.

Read the guide
🏛️ Island

San Giulio Island

A tiny island crowned by a Romanesque basilica and a silent monastery, a two-minute boat ride from Orta's jetty.

The legend of San Giulio
🏛️ UNESCO site

Sacro Monte di Orta

A UNESCO World Heritage hill above the village, with twenty frescoed chapels dedicated to St Francis and sweeping lake views.

🏛️ Sanctuary

Madonna del Sasso

A baroque sanctuary perched on a granite spur, offering arguably the finest panorama over the whole of Lake Orta.

🏛️ Museum

Umbrella & Parasol Museum

In hillside Gignese, the only museum in the world devoted to umbrellas and parasols — quirky, charming and rain-proof.

🌿 Nature, waterfalls & viewpoints

🌿 Waterfall

Cascata della Qualba

A hidden waterfall with an ice-cold natural plunge pool near Boleto — a refreshing reward after a short forest walk.

Cold plunge guide
🌿 Waterfall

Cascata del Pescone

Cascades and green pools along the Pescone stream near Ameno — a lovely spot to cool off on a hot day.

🌿 Viewpoint

Torre di Buccione

A 23-metre medieval watchtower on a wooded knoll, reached by a short walk and rewarding you with a wide view over the southern lake.

🌿 Walk

Monte Mesma Convent

A 15th-century Franciscan convent crowning a quiet, chestnut-covered hill — an easy walk to peace and panorama.

🌿 Hike

Monte Falò & Tre Montagnette

An easy ridge hike above the eastern shore with a string of panoramic viewpoints over lake and Alps.

🌿 Trail

Indigo Ring — WWF Oasis of Ameno

A circular nature trail through the WWF oasis, woodland and evocative abandoned hamlets in the hills of Ameno.

🌿 Viewpoint

Cappella del Vago

A tiny hilltop chapel and one of the loveliest, least-crowded sunset viewpoints over the lake.

🚴 Family fun

Adventure Park Le Pigne

Treetop rope courses and zip-lines in the woods above the lake — a great half-day for families and thrill-seekers.

🚴 Mountain

Mottarone & Alpyland

The 1,491 m peak between Orta and Maggiore, with an alpine coaster, adventure park and a 360° view of seven lakes.
